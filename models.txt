# Grok AI Model - Recommended Models (Flux + A40) - September 2026

## 1. Base Model (required)
Flux.1 Dev
- Preferred: FP8 version (faster, less VRAM)
  Download via script or:
  https://huggingface.co/Kijai/flux-fp8  (flux1-dev-fp8.safetensors)
  or official: https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-dev

Place in: models/unet/

## 2. Text Encoders
- clip_l.safetensors
- t5xxl_fp8_e4m3fn.safetensors  (or t5xxl_fp16 if you want max quality)

From: https://huggingface.co/comfyanonymous/flux_text_encoders
Place in: models/clip/

## 3. VAE
- ae.safetensors
From: black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-dev or FLUX.1-schnell
Place in: models/vae/

## 4. NSFW Unlock LoRA (REQUIRED)
**aidmaNSFWunlock** (most popular, ~19 MB)
- Civitai: https://civitai.com/models/674027
- Trigger word: aidmaNSFWunlock
- Strength: 0.7 – 0.9
- Place in: models/loras/

## 5. Realism / Nude LoRAs (strongly recommended)
- Photorealistic nude v2 (Flux) or "Nude Style for FLUX V2"
- Search Civitai for best current version
- Strength: 0.6 – 0.8

## 6. Character LoRA (after you train it)
- luna23_v1.safetensors (or your chosen trigger)
- Strength: 0.8 – 0.95

## 7. Face Consistency
- IP-Adapter FaceID Plus V2
- InstantID models (via ComfyUI Manager)

## Recommended Generation Settings (Flux)
- Steps: 20–28
- Sampler: euler
- Scheduler: simple
- CFG / Guidance: 2.5 – 3.5
- Resolution: 896×1152 or 1024×1536 (portrait)
